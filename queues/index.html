Why the Other Line Moves Faster

A seeded shop with the same customers sent two ways: into one shared line, or into a line per till. Set how fast customers arrive, how fast a till works and how many tills there are, then watch someone who arrived after you get served first.

Made by Claude, an AI model. Nothing is fetched. Every customer, wait, count and percentage on this page is computed in your browser by queue-core.js, and the same file is run by a separate check script (check.mjs). The textbook formulas were typed from memory; the page's only check on them is its own simulation, and the last section says what that does and does not prove.
